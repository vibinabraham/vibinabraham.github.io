#### Research themes

##### Pacific Northwest National Laboratory (2025–present)

+ **X-ray and core-level spectroscopy:** real-time classical and quantum methods for simulating core (X-ray) spectra of molecules ([J. Chem. Phys. 2026](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jcp/article/164/10/104113/3383265/Elucidating-many-body-effects-in-molecular-core)), and how surrounding electrons screen a core hole ([arXiv:2608.22268](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22268)).
+ **Benchmarking quantum computers for chemistry:** chemically meaningful benchmarks on the path to quantum utility ([arXiv:2601.10813](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10813)), and uncertainty quantification across noisy quantum hardware ([arXiv:2607.14392](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14392)).
+ **Hybrid quantum–classical many-body methods:** a combined quantum–classical formulation that improves accuracy when the correlation model is incomplete, while keeping a variational bound on the energy ([arXiv:2609.04387](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04387)).

##### University of Michigan, postdoc with Dominika Zgid (2022–2024)

+ **Relativistic Green's function (GW) methods for heavy elements:** fully self-consistent GW for accurate ionization potentials and total energies of heavy-element molecules, up to actinides ([JCTC 2024](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/acs.jctc.4c00075), [J. Phys. Chem. Lett. 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31571)).
+ **Many-body methods for solids and condensed matter:** finite-temperature, fully self-consistent GW for periodic solids, including relativistic effects ([Faraday Discuss. 2024](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2024/fd/d4fd00043a)), implemented in the open-source [Green](https://green-phys.org/) package ([Comput. Phys. Commun. 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0010465524003035)).

##### Virginia Tech, PhD with Nicholas Mayhall (2016–2021)

+ **Tensor product state methods for strong correlation:** cluster-based wavefunctions (TPSCI, cluster many-body expansion) that make strongly correlated systems tractable ([JCTC 2020](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/acs.jctc.0c00141)).
+ **Singlet fission for solar energy:** design rules for triplet-pair states in covalent dimers.
+ **Quantum algorithms for excited states:** equation-of-motion methods on near-term quantum computers ([Chem. Sci. 2023](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2023/sc/d2sc05371c)).
